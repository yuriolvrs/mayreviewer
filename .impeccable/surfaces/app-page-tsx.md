# Landing (/) — first-time visitors

Mode: Persuade. Shown on "/" to visitors with no reviewers, no session, and no prior app entry (EntryScript); everyone else gets the reviewers home.
Audience: Filipino college students, phone first, often the night before an exam.
Action: "Make my reviewer, free" → /reviewer/new. Secondary: Log in.
Proof: the product's own question formats, rendered and answerable. No testimonials, counts, or ratings exist; none are claimed. Sample items are labeled as a sample.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brand line is a real message; the reply is a practice exam. Refuses the headline-over-subcopy-over-feature-cards template.
OWN-WORLD: May Reviewer's world unchanged: bg #f7f7f6, white bordered paper, warm accent #a8681f only on the sent bubble, the CTA and selection; Libre Franklin at very large display vs small uppercase labels; Space Mono only inside the timeline table and code listing. Test-paper grammar: Name/Score rule, "Test I–V" headings with Directions lines, drawn blanks.
STORY: Recognize the group-chat moment → see the reviewer is a real exam in your prof's format → answer items, watch the score fill → read the directions (how it works) → make your own.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Timestamp, received bubble "pre, may reviewer ka ba?", sent accent bubble "meron, eto oh", then the attached paper: Name/Score header, two-line headline at max scale, the CTA under the headline, then the answerable Test I timeline item starting below it.
FORM: Chat → Test Paper, fusion of ranked candidates 2 and 4; seed key 56167a2a. Signature interaction: the paper's Score line is sticky and fills as the visitor answers Tests I–V.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
